Move the beads yourself

This interactive practice uses the school’s rod layout: counting from the right, the first two rods are reserved for decimal work, and the third rod from the right is the red-marked unit rod. The tens rod is immediately to its left, then hundreds and thousands.

How to move a bead: Tap a bead to move it toward or away from the reckoning bar. On each rod, the upper bead is worth 5 and each lower bead is worth 1. Beads touching the bar are counted. Use the physical abacus alongside this screen for beginners.

Learning rules
