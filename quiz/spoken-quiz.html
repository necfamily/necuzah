The numbers will be spoken as a mathematical expression, for example: “Three plus four, minus two.”

Listen until the expression is complete. Then work out the answer using your mental mathematics skills.

Our goal is to make this experience feel like a real teacher guiding you, with natural speech, a comfortable thinking pause, and an encouraging voice.
