Physical-abacus model

Move the beads. Understand the value.

This model follows your instrument: the two rods to the right of the unit rod are reserved for decimals. The third rod from the right is the unit rod. Its first lower bead next to the reckoning bar is red; its upper bead remains yellow.

Bead rule: upper bead = 5. Each lower bead = 1. A bead counts only when moved towards the reckoning bar.
Interactive abacus

Tap a bead to move it

Tap an upper bead to move it to or away from the bar. Tap a lower bead to move that bead and the lower beads nearer the bar together. The highlighted rod is the unit rod.

Place-value practice

Build 7 and 11

For 7, use the unit rod: the upper bead (5) and two lower beads (1 + 1) are active. For 11, show 1 on the tens rod and 1 on the unit rod. Do not make 11 by adding 5 and 2 on one rod.
